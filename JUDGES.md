# Judging Pathlight in 5 minutes

**Hosted app:** https://pathlight.up.railway.app
**Sign-in:** use the email and password in the Devpost **testing instructions** field. They are not in this repository.
**Track:** Ring (with AWS Builder: Amazon Bedrock and Amazon SES)

The judge account opens **Demo café**. This is a real coffee-shop floor plan (CC0) turned into a TwinForge twin:
- **Front door**, **Parking lot** and **Counter** are paired with real Ring cameras.
- The other map cameras are marked **SIM**.
- Two weeks of customers are simulated so the analytics have something to show. They are labelled **Simulated** everywhere.
- Visits from the real Ring cameras are labelled **Live**.

The judge account is isolated: it has no access to the owner's Ring account, home or other places.

## The path

| # | Do this | You should see |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sign in. **Home** opens on Demo café. | The café floor plan in **2D** with camera wedges and the business areas: entrance, counter, window bar, seating, lounge, parking. |
| 2 | Tap the **people** button on the map (**Show people heatmap**). Change the time span. | The people heatmap: where customers actually walk and wait. Counter and entrance glow brightest; corners stay cool. |
| 3 | Switch to **3D** and drag to orbit. | The same twin as an architectural model, with Ring hardware models mounted on the walls. |
| 4 | Open **Analytics** (Site Analytics). | Visits, peak hour and busiest zone at the top. Below: visits per day against last week, visits by hour, a busy-times grid (darker is busier), zones with average time spent, and entrances. |
| 5 | Toggle **Live only / All data**. Scroll to **Vehicles**. | Live only counts just the real Ring visits, including the week imported from Ring Event History; All data adds the simulated weeks. Vehicles counts Ring Smart Alerts `vehicle` events by hour, separately from visits. |
| 6 | Read **Weekly insight**. | A short plain-English summary written by **Amazon Bedrock (Nova Pro)** from aggregate numbers only (no images, no people). The line under it says the note was checked: every figure matches the data, following Ring's Computer Vision Guidelines. |
| 7 | Look at **Queue & crowding alerts → Recent alerts**. | A counter queue alert raised when the counter zone passed its limit. The owner can change each zone's limit or turn it off. |
| 8 | Property management: switch places with the **Sites** tabs in Analytics, and open **Settings → Places & floor plans**. | One account runs several places, each with its own twin, cameras and analytics. A new place starts from an uploaded floor plan (optional Bedrock vision ignores furniture). |
| 9 | Open **Activity** and pick a **Live** visit from the **Front door** Ring camera. | The Ring recording plays beside the map while the detected path grows on the floor plan. The person is shown as an anonymous floor point; the recording is not stored. |

## What's real and what's simulated

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Live** | From a physical Ring camera through the Ring Partner API: signed webhook, then recording, then the person's path |
| **Simulated** | Generated by demo mode so the café looks busy; no camera saw it |
| **SIM** camera | A map camera not yet paired with a Ring device |
| *Estimated* position | Derived from camera placement and field of view; not a calibrated measurement |

## If something looks off

- **"AI insights aren't set up on this server":** the Bedrock key is missing on the deployment. Contact the maintainer (below).
- **The map is empty after sign-in:** pick **Demo café** in the top bar.
- **A live visit says "Ring is still preparing this recording":** Ring makes recordings available a short time after the event. Retry after a minute.
- **The service is down:** check `/health` and `/status` on the hosted URL.

Want to run it locally? See the [README](README.md#run-it-yourself). Ring integration details are in the [friction log](FRICTION_LOG.md).
